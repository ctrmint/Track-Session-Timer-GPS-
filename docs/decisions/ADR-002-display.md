# ADR-002: Prototype on Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.41-B

**Status:** Accepted for prototype, not yet production-frozen

## Decision

Use the cased 2.41 inch Waveshare ESP32-S3 AMOLED board as the first integrated controller/display.

## Reasons

- materially larger than the original 1.28 inch display
- 600 x 450 resolution
- stated 800 cd/m2 brightness
- onboard IMU, RTC, microSD and touch
- 8 MB PSRAM and 16 MB flash
- supplied case accelerates prototype work

## Validation gate

The choice is not final until a direct sunlight cockpit test confirms acceptable readability.
